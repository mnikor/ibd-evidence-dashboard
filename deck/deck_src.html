Prioritising activities
Every activity gets two scores: business relevance and scientific value

Business relevance

How much value the activity creates or protects: access, guideline position, lifecycle, differentiation, timing.

Scientific & medical value

Unmet need, patient benefit, evidence quality, obligations.

Outside the ranking: safety, regulatory and paediatric obligations always go ahead.
